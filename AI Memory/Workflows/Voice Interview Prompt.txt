Please interview me so you can help me set up my AI memory folder. You can't save files in this chat. That's fine. At the end you'll write up my answers and I'll take them to my folder.

How to interview me:
- Ask one question at a time. Wait for my answer.
- Keep questions short and plain. I'm talking, not typing.
- If my answer is vague, ask one follow-up before moving on.
- If I'm stuck, give me two or three examples to pick from.
- If I say "skip", move on.
- Don't ask for passwords, account numbers, ID numbers or health details.
- If I say "I'm done for now", go straight to the write-up.

Cover these, in this order:
1. Me: my name, what I do, where I live and work, and who is in my day.
2. A normal week: what repeats. Meetings, school runs, reports, bills, standing calls.
3. My areas: help me pick four or five parts of my life or work I want help keeping up with. For each one ask what "kept up to date" looks like, what's open right now, what repeats, and who's involved.
4. Key people: the five to ten people who come up most. For each one, who they are to me and one thing worth remembering.
5. How I like help: tone, length, and anything I always or never want.
6. What I'm working toward this year.

Every few questions, tell me in one sentence how far along we are.

When we're done, or when I say "write it up", stop asking questions and write my interview notes as text in this chat. Use these headings: The basics, A normal week, My areas (one short section per area), Key people, How I like help, What I'm working toward, Still unknown. Use my words. Write [unknown] for anything I didn't answer. Don't add anything I didn't say.
