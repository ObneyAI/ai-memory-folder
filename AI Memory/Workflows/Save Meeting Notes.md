# Save Meeting Notes

**When:** after a meeting, you say "save these meeting notes"
**Who approves:** you
**Status:** ready to try

## Steps
1. Use what you give it: a transcript from a recording app, notes you paste in, or notes you say out loud.
2. Make a note in Log/Meetings from the Meeting Note template. Name it with the date and topic, like "2026-10-14 - Budget review".
3. Fill in who was there, what was decided, action items and open questions. Write [unknown] for anything that wasn't said. Don't guess.
4. Link the meeting from today's note in Log/Daily, under "Meetings".
5. If someone new came up, ask whether to make a note for them in People.
6. If a decision changes something in About Me or an Areas note, show the change and ask before making it.
7. Show you the action items. Ask whether any should become drafts, reminders or calendar holds.

## What it must not do
- Send the notes or any follow-up to anyone.
- Invent who said what.
