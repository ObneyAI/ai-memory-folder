# Workflows

Each note here is one job your assistant does for you. Every workflow follows the same rule: the assistant drafts, you approve, and nothing is sent, posted or deleted without your yes.

Two prompts you can copy into another chat:
- **Voice Interview Prompt.txt**: the interview, for voice mode.
- **Memory Export Prompt.txt**: asks ChatGPT or Claude chat what it already knows about you.
