# Morning Check

**When:** you say "run the morning check"
**Who approves:** you
**Status:** ready to try

## Steps
1. Open today's note in Log/Daily. If it isn't there, make it from the Daily Note template. Name it with today's date, like 2026-10-14.
2. Read yesterday's note. Carry over anything under "Needs you" that isn't done. On Mondays, also read the "Next week" list in the newest note in Log/Weekly.
3. If your calendar and email are connected, read today's calendar and the email since yesterday. If they aren't, ask you what's on today.
4. Check the "Open right now" list in each note in Areas. Pick out anything due this week.
5. List what needs you today: replies you owe, decisions, deadlines this week. Put the list under "Needs you" in today's note. Keep it short. Five items is plenty.
6. Ask which items you'd like replies drafted for.

## What it must not do
- Send, delete or archive any email.
- Accept or decline a meeting.
- Add anything to your calendar without asking.
