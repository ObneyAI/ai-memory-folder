# Start Here

This is your AI memory folder. Your assistant keeps notes here about you, the people in your life, the parts of life and work you care about, and what happens each day.

Because the notes live in a folder on your computer, they belong to you. Any AI tool that can open this folder can read them.

## Work in Cowork, not chat

When you're at your computer, open the Claude desktop app, go to Cowork, and open your AI Memory project. That's how your assistant reads and writes these notes.

A regular chat can't see this folder. If you ask a chat about your notes, it will tell you to open Cowork. Chat and voice mode are still fine for anything else, and for the voice version of the interview.

## Where things live

- **About Me**: who you are, your areas, a normal week, the people who matter, how you like help
- **Areas**: one note for each part of life or work you want help keeping up with
- **People**: one note per person
- **Organizations**: one note per company, school, church, club or group
- **Log**: a note for each day, each meeting and each week
- **Inbox**: anything new that hasn't been sorted yet
- **Workflows**: the jobs you can ask for, written down step by step
- **Templates**: blank forms your assistant copies from

## What you can say

- **"Interview me"**: start here. Your assistant asks you questions, one at a time, and writes your first notes. About 30 minutes. You can stop and pick it up later.
- **"Add this to today"**: it goes in today's note.
- **"Save these meeting notes"**: after a meeting, paste or say what happened.
- **"Run the morning check"**: see what needs you today.
- **"Reflect"**: once a week, look back, update your notes and tidy up.

You can also just ask a question. Your assistant reads this folder first.

Nothing gets sent, posted or deleted until you say yes.

## Your first week

1. Say "interview me".
2. Each morning, say "run the morning check".
3. After meetings, say "save these meeting notes".
4. On Friday, say "reflect".
